This week, we start our new unit by exploring different types of bullying and what we can do to get help.

 

Our success criteria for the lesson are:

I can identify offensive and illegal online behavior, such as cyberbullying, that can harm others.

I understand the importance of reporting offensive and harmful online behavior to protect myself and others.

I understand the importance of privacy settings and knowing who I am interacting with online to ensure my safety during digital activities.

Our success criteria for the lesson are:

I understand that different people may interpret my online posts differently based on their perspectives and experiences.

I understand the importance of reporting offensive or harmful incidents on digital platforms to ensure a safer online environment.

I can describe strategies to safeguard my online presence and seek guidance from trusted adults to ensure my safety and wellbeing in the digital world.

I am confident in incorporating different multimedia elements such as sound, video, and text to enhance the quality of my digital projects
